Exchange & API keys

The bot trades ANDX derivatives — real longs and shorts with leverage (set by the "Max leverage" risk field), and stop-loss/take-profit enforced by the exchange itself. Candles, quotes, orders, and balances all come from ANDX's API; only the key and secret are needed.
Keys are stored only on this machine (secrets.json, owner-read-only) and never leave it except to call your exchange. Create keys with trade permission only — never enable withdrawals.

Bot settings

Everyone starts from the same bot — you compete by editing the strategy (the Edit Strategy tab, or strategies.py). This tag just labels your entry.
Practice progress carries across stops and restarts. Reset (or change the starting balance) to begin fresh; trade history is kept.

Edit Strategy — your bot's brain

This is strategies.py, where the buy/sell decisions live. Change the logic, hit Save & Apply, and your bot runs your code. It's checked for errors first — broken code is refused, so you can't brick your bot. A good place to start: the numbers in EMATrend (fast/slow moving averages) or RSIMeanReversion (the RSI buy/sell levels). Test in paper mode.
